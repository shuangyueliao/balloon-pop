# Balloon Pop — Store Submission Materials

## Short description (CrazyGames / GameDistribution / itch.io)

Pop rising balloons before they float away! Big 1pt, small 3pt, gold 5pt, chain blasts, +3s clocks, spikes and bees. Swipe combos up to x3 in 60s of fun.

## Details

- Genre: Arcade / Casual / Clicker
- Controls: Mouse / Touch. Tap to pop, or hold & swipe to pop every balloon
  along the path. Combos build when you keep popping; tapping empty sky
  resets the combo multiplier.
- Features: 60-second timed rounds, big 1pt / small 3pt / gold 5pt balloons,
  chain blast balloons, +3s clock balloons, spike balls (-5 with screen
  shake), bees that steal balloons, best score & best combo saving, mute
  toggle, procedural graphics & sounds, single-file HTML5 (no external
  assets), works offline once loaded.
- Tech: Vanilla JavaScript, Canvas, WebAudio. No frameworks, no tracking,
  no external links.
